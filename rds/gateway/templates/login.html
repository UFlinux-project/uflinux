HOW IT WORKS

Everything in its place.

The gateway holds the session, not your data. Credentials go to PAM once, the token is spent on the first frame, and the stream ends when you close the tab.

01 /

One sign-in

Your distro account is the only account. PAM checks it, and the same password unlocks the remote desktop.

02 /

Nothing to install

The desktop is decoded in the browser by the gateway's own WebAssembly module. No plugin, no download, no third-party script.

03 /

Gone when you go

Closing the tab disconnects the session and burns the token. Nothing is kept on the server between visits.

Simple by design.

Alpine Linux + labwc, served from a machine you control.
